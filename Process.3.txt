### Data Cleaning & Preparation Documentation

**1. `dailyActivity_merged` Dataset:**

* **Duplicates Removal:** Removed 45 duplicate rows using full-row validation (`Select all`), retaining 895 unique daily tracking records.
* **Data Integrity Check:** Verified complete alignment between `TotalDistance` and `TrackerDistance`, confirming full reliance on automated GPS/step tracking rather than manual inputs.
* **Sheet Cleanup:** Deleted 11 empty, unutilized columns (`Column 1` through `Column 11`) and removed temporary conditional formatting rules.
* **Formatting Standardization:** Standardized all distance metrics to two decimal places for numerical consistency.

**2. `sleepDay_merged` Dataset:**

* **Duplicates Removal:** Identified and eliminated 3 full duplicate rows, retaining 411 unique sleep records.
* **Date Format Standardization:** Standardized the `SleepDay` column to a clean Date format by removing the redundant static timestamp (`12:00:00 AM`).
* **Chronological Sorting:** Applied an ascending sort (A to Z) on `SleepDay` to align tracking dates sequentially for time-series analysis.
