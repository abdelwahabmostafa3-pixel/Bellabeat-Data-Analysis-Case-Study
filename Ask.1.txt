## 1. Ask Phase

### Business Task:
Analyze smart device usage data to uncover trends and patterns in daily activity, sedentary behavior, and sleep habits among non-Bellabeat smart tracker users. The insights gained will be applied to guide strategic marketing decisions and enhance product positioning for Bellabeat's wellness products.

### Key Stakeholders:
- Urška Sršen: Co-founder and Chief Creative Officer of Bellabeat.
- Sando Mur: Co-founder and key member of the executive team.
- Bellabeat Marketing Analytics Team.

### Core Business Questions:
1. What are the key trends in daily activity, steps, and sedentary behaviors among smart device users?
2. How do these usage trends apply to Bellabeat's target audience?
3. How can these insights inform Bellabeat’s marketing strategy and feature recommendations?
